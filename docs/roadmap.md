# Roadmap

Milestones end with a check-in: results, images, and the next decisions.

| Milestone | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **N0: Foundation** | Repository, CI, the founding decision ([ADR 0002](adr/0002-founding-nodule.md)), agatesim as a pinned library, the literature catalogue carried over | done |
| **N1: Taxonomy** | A faceted taxonomy of agate forms: banding style, body shape, internal structures, optical effects; fortification as a case of wall-parallel banding; candidates (lace, fire, others) only with a source; how specimens and results are tagged on each facet (ADR 0003) | next |
| **N2: Architecture and a 2D trial** | The shared state (chemistry, precursor, fabric per cell), how processes run together on it, the two banding drivers, claim grades in the output; a 2D trial of three couplings (both drivers on one chemistry, a fabric-based milkiness rule, settling of particles grown during lining), with time and memory measured | planned |
| **N3: Ageing and optics from fabric** | Crystallite growth and moganite loss with age; milkiness, shadow and iris derived from fabric | planned |
| **N4: Mixtures against the library** | Simulated nodules judged by the mixtures of forms in the reference library, through agatesim's classifier and feature maps | planned |
