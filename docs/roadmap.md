# Roadmap

Milestones end with a check-in: results, images, and the next decisions. The
order follows the goals in [ADR 0003](adr/0003-scope-a-3d-specimen-simulator.md):
image synthesis, then realistic specimens, then hypothesis testing.

| Milestone | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **N0: Foundation** | Repository, CI, the founding decision ([ADR 0002](adr/0002-founding-nodule.md)), agatesim as a pinned library, the literature catalogue carried over | done |
| **N1: Scope and characterization schema** | The owner's [scope brief](scope.md) as ADR 0003. A reading pass on the gaps (exterior morphology and weathering, quantitative band descriptors, 3D imaging of agate, stereology), done. [ADR 0004](adr/0004-characterization-schema-core-set.md), accepted: a core set of 22 descriptors measurable from photos now, slots for those needing slab pairs or 3D, a section-sampling model per photo set. [ADR 0006](adr/0006-band-counts-as-censored-records.md), accepted: band counts as lower bounds with the photo's resolving limit, three descriptors from the owner's notes, a render-and-compare fallback, taken 2026-10-31 (section 10). The reference library re-tagged on the domains | done |
| **N2: Representation and a 3D vertical slice** | [ADR 0005](adr/0005-representation-implicit-hybrid.md), accepted 2026-10-31: an implicit specimen with coarse world-fixed grids, fractures as reverse-time cut-and-offset operators, bands as a record over growth time, and the six subsystems' interfaces. One simple nodule end to end: a lobed exterior, wall-parallel bands, one inclusion type, one fracture; complete in 3D, cut anywhere, rendered; descriptors measured on its cuts and on library photos by the same code, each with a negative control. Time and memory measured. Renderer: [ADR 0007](adr/0007-renderer-cut-face-monte-carlo.md), accepted 2026-11-01 (GPU Monte Carlo; cut face in reflected light plus a backlit slab). Set-level compare and blind test: [ADR 0008](adr/0008-set-level-render-and-compare.md), accepted 2026-11-01. Then sub-resolution structure (ADR 0009), optics tuning in four steps (ADRs 0010, 0012-0014), band regularity and order (ADRs 0011, 0015, 0017) and materials that vary along the bands (ADR 0016), all accepted by 2026-11-06 | done |
| **N3: Image synthesis** | Procedural generators for exterior, banding, inclusions, defects and colour (domains A, B, C, E, H); optics in physical units, so appearance depends on lighting, thickness and finish; a realism score against the library (descriptors, classifier, feature maps). Under way since 2026-11-06 as broad mock-ups of many features varied per stone, judged by the owner's eye on preview sheets and renders ([ADR 0018](adr/0018-the-variety-gap-broad-mock-ups.md), [ADR 0019](adr/0019-rendering-the-variety-layers.md)). Built so far: outlines including thunder eggs, quartz and open centres, floors, warp, band roughness, cracks, mottle, dust, plumes, moss, early crystals, host rind and specks, escape tubes. Order of the remaining work: [workplan.md](workplan.md); its feature items ended 2026-11-21 (the last, the face's finish, closed without a build: [ADR 0020](adr/0020-the-cut-face-stays-polished.md)). The realism score waits until the variety work pauses (owner, ADR 0018 s.5) | in progress |
| **N4: Realistic specimens** | Architecture, texture, composition, anisotropy and multiscale structure (D, F, G, I, J); whole nodules whose random cuts and exteriors match real distributions; cut and polished surfaces vs natural ones | planned |
| **N5: Hypothesis testing** | agatesim ADR 0072's coupled-process programme as mechanistic generators in the same representation: shared chemistry, two banding drivers, fabric per cell, ageing, optics from fabric; graded claims; mixtures of forms against the library | planned |
| **Data track** (alongside) | Reference data in ADR 0003's priority order: library re-tagging, exterior photos paired with cuts, the owner's specimens under a capture protocol, published measurements, 3D data; each new external source asked first | ongoing |
